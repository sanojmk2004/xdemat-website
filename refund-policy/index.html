Refund & Cancellation Policy

How refunds and cancellations work for payments made to XDemat for XRover 5 trial packs, monthly subscriptions and training courses.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Refund PolicyCancellation Policy

Scope

This policy applies to payments made directly to XDemat for:

  • XRover 5 trial packs (3 trading days of access).
  • XRover 5 monthly subscriptions, including access to the invite-only TradingView indicators, signal notifications and training videos that come with the plan.
  • Training courses offered by XDemat, such as the XRover 5 Strategy Course.

Account opening with Angel One: XDemat does not charge you for account-opening assistance. Any account charges, brokerage, deposits or withdrawals are handled by Angel One under Angel One's own terms and policies, and refunds for them must be requested from Angel One.

Refund Policy

Our products are digital services. Access to the indicators, signals and training videos is given to you shortly after payment and cannot be returned once used. For this reason, payments are non-refundable once access has been activated, except in the cases listed below.

When you are eligible for a refund

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged twice for the same plan.
  • Wrong amount: you were charged more than the published price of the plan you chose. The extra amount is refunded.
  • Access not activated: you paid, but we could not activate your access within 2 trading days of confirming your payment.
  • Cancelled before activation: you cancelled before your access was activated (see the Cancellation Policy below).
  • Service not available from us: XDemat is unable to provide the plan or course you paid for.

When refunds are not given

  • After access to a trial pack, subscription or course has been activated, including if you stop using it partway through the period.
  • For trading losses, missed trades or results that did not meet your expectations. XRover 5 is an analysis tool and does not guarantee profits.
  • For problems caused by your own setup, such as your TradingView account, internet connection, broker platform or device.
  • When access is suspended or cancelled because of a breach of our Terms & Conditions, such as sharing your access, reselling signals or copying our materials.

How to request a refund

  1. Email info@xdemat.com within 7 days of the payment.
  2. Include your name, the mobile number registered with us, the plan you paid for, the payment date, and the transaction ID or a payment screenshot.
  3. Tell us the reason for your request.

Cancellation Policy

All plans are prepaid for a fixed period. There is no automatic renewal or auto-debit. You renew only when you choose to make a new payment.

Access periods

PlanPeriod
Trial pack3 trading days
Monthly subscription1 month, counted in trading days
Training courseThe access period shown when you buy the course

Trading days are Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are not counted. If your access is given after 9:30 AM, your period starts on the next trading day.

How to cancel

  • To stop your subscription: simply do not renew it. Your access ends automatically at the end of the period you paid for. You can also tell us by email or WhatsApp so we stop sending renewal reminders.
  • Before access is activated: email info@xdemat.com or message us on WhatsApp before your access is activated, and we will cancel the order and refund the full amount.
  • After access is activated: you can cancel at any time, and your access continues until the end of the paid period. No refund is given for the remaining days.

Cancellation by XDemat

We may suspend or cancel access without a refund if you break our Terms & Conditions, for example by sharing your login or indicator access, reselling or redistributing signals, or copying our training material. If we cancel a plan for any other reason, we will refund the unused portion of your payment.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page, with the date shown at the top, applies to payments made on or after that date.
